Search that finds

Results come in groups, each in Bible order. Each group says how its verses were found: the exact words, other forms of the word, all your words, some of them, the Hebrew or Greek words behind your word, and the headings the BSB's publisher printed. A word also lists the Hebrew and Greek words behind it, and what the commentaries you have added say about it.

Press a word. See the word under it.

Long-press perfect in Matthew 5:48 and Berea opens τέλειος, teleios: what it means, all 19 of its uses across 9 books, how each is translated, and every verse.

One English word can stand for several. The King James Version’s perfect in Job 1:1 is תָּם, tam, which the Berean Standard Bible translates blameless. In the Berean Standard Bible each word is matched exactly, from its translators’ own tables; in other translations Berea matches by meaning and says so.

The app

It opens on the Bible. Plans for reading, Search in the middle, Prayer for what you are asking God, Notes for everything you keep.

All of it on the device, none of it on a server.

    Your data, exactly.

    Everything Berea stores, and everything it sends.

    Stored on your phone

    The Bible library above. One file for what you write: notes and the notebooks they sit in, the readings you add to a notebook, highlights, reading plans and prayers. Translations you import, and your settings.

    Sent over the internet

    Nothing, unless you tap Download: the optional Greek lexicon and the commentary packs come from GitHub, each checked against its fingerprint before it is kept.

    Backups

    Berea opts out of Android and iCloud backup on purpose. Keep your own backup instead: one file with your notes, notebooks, readings and prayers, encrypted with a passphrase only you know (AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2 at 600,000 rounds).
