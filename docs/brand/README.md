# Berea brand mark: Lamp b

The mark is a lowercase b (stem and bowl) with a lamp flame above the stem.
These files are masters for design and store listings. They are not bundled into the app.

## Files

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `berea-mark.svg` | Mark on light backgrounds (Parchment colours), transparent |
| `berea-mark-night.svg` | Mark on dark backgrounds (Night colours), transparent |
| `berea-mark-mono.svg` | One-colour mark; takes `currentColor` (print, stamps, notification-style uses) |
| `berea-app-icon.svg` | App icon master: Night square tile, mark at 65.56% of the side |
| `berea-app-icon-1024.png` | 1024 px app icon (App Store, general marketing) |
| `google-play-icon-512.png` | 512 px Google Play listing icon, full bleed (Play applies its own mask) |

The platform icons themselves live in `android/`, `ios/`, `macos/` and `windows/`.

## Colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night | `#161310` | Icon tile, dark backgrounds |
| Linen ink | `#EAE2D4` | b on Night |
| Gold flame | `#D4A94E` | Flame on Night |
| Parchment | `#F6F1E8` | Light backgrounds |
| Ink | `#1E1A15` | b on Parchment |
| Deep gold flame | `#9E7628` | Flame on Parchment |

## Rules

- The b is ink (on light) or linen (on dark); the flame is always gold.
- Where two colours cannot print, use one colour for the whole mark.
- Never outline, rotate, stretch or recolour the mark, add rays or glow, or move the flame.
  The open gap between flame and stem is part of the mark.
- Clear space: at least 24 units (on the 100-unit grid) on every side.
- Smallest size: 16 px. At 16 to 24 px the mark may fill up to 78% of its tile.
- Proportions: square tiles 65.56% of the side; round masks 57.8% of the diameter.
